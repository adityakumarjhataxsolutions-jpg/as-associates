FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our GST, Income Tax, TDS, accounting and compliance services in New Delhi.

What services does A.S. & ASSOCIATES offer?

A.S. & ASSOCIATES offers GST services, Income Tax and ITR filing, TDS returns, accounting and bookkeeping, Tally accounting, business registration, audit support, MIS and reporting, tax notice support, and MCA/ROC and DSC support.

Where is A.S. & ASSOCIATES located?

We are based in New Delhi. Online coordination is also available for document-based work.

Do you provide GST registration and GST return filing?

Yes. We help with GST registration, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B filing, GSTR-9 annual return, GSTR-2B reconciliation and e-way bill support.

Can you help with Income Tax Return (ITR) filing?

Yes. We support individuals, professionals and businesses with ITR filing, tax computation, tax planning support, business ITR, tax audit support and outstanding demand support.

Do you handle TDS return filing?

Yes. Our TDS services include TDS return filing, computation, reconciliation, Form 16 and 16A support and TDS compliance. A dedicated TDS and compliance contact is available at +91 73037 53885.

Do you provide accounting, bookkeeping and Tally services?

Yes. We offer bookkeeping, Tally accounting, billing and invoicing, bank reconciliation, profit and loss statements and balance sheets.

Can you help with company, LLP or OPC registration?

Yes. We support company, LLP and OPC registration, MSME/Udyam registration, FSSAI support and other business licenses.

Can you help with GST or Income Tax notices?

Yes. We provide GST notice support, Income Tax notice support, GST demand support, compliance review and documentation support. Contact our team with your notice so we can understand the requirement.

How much do your services cost?

Fees depend on the type of work, your business and the filing volume. Please call or WhatsApp +91 62058 96093 with your requirement and our team will share the fee.

How can I contact A.S. & ASSOCIATES?

Call or WhatsApp +91 62058 96093 (main contact), call +91 87898 51415 for general enquiries, or email adityakumarjha.taxsolutions@gmail.com. You can also use the Service Request form on this page.
